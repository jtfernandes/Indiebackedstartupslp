# IndieBacked Startups — V2 Copy Deck

**Status:** draft for review · all copy editable · placeholders flagged
**Voice:** founder-to-founder, dry confidence, specific over adjective-y
**Languages:** EN + PT, parallel from day one

---

## Voice rules (apply everywhere)

- One promise per sentence
- Specifics beat adjectives ("11 carriers" not "many carriers")
- Founder-to-founder, never marketer-to-buyer
- Active voice; subject is the founder when possible
- Numbers are anchors — renewal dates, carrier count, stage tiers, prices
- **Banned words:** revolutionary, empowering, next-generation, AI-powered, seamless, holistic, ecosystem, journey, leverage (as a verb), unlock

## 3 · How autopilot runs

**Header** · `Set it once. Stay covered.`
**PT** · `Configuras uma vez. Continuas coberto.`

**Sub**
> No quote forms. No insurance vocab. No annual scramble.

**PT** · *Sem formulários. Sem jargão. Sem corrida anual.*

**Three steps** — each ~30 words.

**01 · Tell us once.**
30 minutes of questions. Stage, headcount, customers, geography. The kind of thing your CFO already knows.
*PT — **Conta-nos uma vez.** 30 minutos de perguntas. Fase, headcount, clientes, geografias. Coisas que o teu CFO já sabe de cor.*

**02 · We pick and bind.**
We shop 11 carriers across Portugal and Spain, propose a stack that fits your stage, and bind the policies you approve. Usually within a week.
*PT — **Escolhemos e contratamos.** Comparamos 11 seguradoras em Portugal e Espanha, propomos um stack adequado à tua fase e contratamos as apólices que aprovares. Normalmente numa semana.*

**03 · We renew before you notice.**
We track your renewals, your headcount, your funding rounds, and your customer geographies. When something changes, we update your stack and tell you what changed. You don't chase us.
*PT — **Renovamos antes de reparares.** Acompanhamos as renovações, o teu headcount, as tuas rondas e as geografias dos teus clientes. Quando algo muda, atualizamos o stack e dizemos-te o que mudou. Não és tu que andas atrás de nós.*

## 5 · The risk stack

**Header** · `Running quietly in the background.`
**PT** · `A correr em segundo plano.`

**Sub**
> What's actually in your stack — without the insurance vocab.

**PT** · *O que está mesmo no teu stack — sem o jargão dos seguros.*

**Six rows.** Each: name (one word), one-line plain-English explainer, trigger that activates it.

| Coverage | Explainer (EN) | Activates when |
|---|---|---|
| **D&O** | Personal lawsuits against your board and execs. | You have a board, or your investors require it. |
| **Cyber** | Ransomware, breach disclosure, downtime. | You store customer data or process payments. |
| **Tech E&O** | What your product does — or fails to do — for customers. | You sell software or a service to other businesses. |
| **EPL** | Wrongful termination, harassment, discrimination claims. | You have employees, not just contractors. |
| **AT + Saúde** | Workers' comp (mandatory in PT/ES) + group health. | You hire your first employee. |
| **AI Liability** | What your AI product does or recommends. New under the EU AI Act. | Your product uses AI in any decision the customer relies on. |

**PT — same rows, translated:**

| Cobertura | Explicação | Quando ativa |
|---|---|---|
| **D&O** | Processos pessoais contra o board e os executivos. | Tens um board, ou os investidores exigem. |
| **Ciber** | Ransomware, divulgação de breach, downtime. | Guardas dados de clientes ou processas pagamentos. |
| **Tech E&O** | O que o teu produto faz — ou deixa de fazer — para os clientes. | Vendes software ou serviço a outras empresas. |
| **EPL** | Despedimento sem justa causa, assédio, discriminação. | Tens colaboradores, não só freelancers. |
| **AT + Saúde** | Acidentes de trabalho (obrigatório em PT/ES) + saúde de grupo. | Contratas o primeiro funcionário. |
| **AI Liability** | O que o teu produto de IA faz ou recomenda. Novo com o AI Act. | O teu produto usa IA em decisões para o cliente. |
